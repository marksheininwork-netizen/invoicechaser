# App Store Connect listing draft — InvoiceChaser

- App name: InvoiceChaser
- Subtitle: Friendly invoice follow-ups
- Bundle ID: `com.mariasheinin.invoicechaser`
- Version: 1.0.0 (build 1)
- Category: Business
- Promotional text: Keep unpaid invoices visible and prepare a thoughtful reminder when it is time to follow up.
- Description: InvoiceChaser is a simple invoice desk for independent service businesses. Add invoices with client, amount, due date, and optional email. Search and filter your list, see past-due and upcoming balances, and export records as CSV. When a follow-up is due, prepare an email draft for your review.

Your demo data is stored on this device in your browser storage. Nothing is sent automatically and InvoiceChaser does not process payments. This early-access version does not sync data between devices or provide a hosted business account.
