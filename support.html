Help with your invoice desk.

Invoices are saved in the browser and device where you created them. Use Export CSV before clearing site data or moving to another device.

Reminder actions prepare a draft only. Review it in your email app before sending.

For help or early access, contact the project on GitHub. GitHub issues are public. Never post invoices, customer names or contact details, tax or payment information, passwords, or confidential records. Use a redacted or synthetic example and describe your device and problem.
