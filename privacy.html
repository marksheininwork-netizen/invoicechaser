Your invoice data stays yours.

InvoiceChaser stores invoices in this browser on this device. The app has no account, server, analytics, advertising, or payment processing.

CSV export creates a file on your device. Reminder buttons prepare text for you to review; opening an email draft is your choice and sends nothing. Requesting early access opens GitHub and shares only what you choose to submit there.

Clearing this browser’s site data removes locally saved invoices. Export a CSV first if you want a backup.

Questions: use the Request early access link in the app and include only information you choose to share.
